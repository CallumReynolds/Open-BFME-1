## Hypothesis and measured result

The preferred bank independently reproduced 308 bytes with 14 differences. The remaining instructions were the initial zero comparison, the order of the two zero stores, and an ESI/EDI mirror. Declaration order, local references, coordinate temporaries, scoped lifetimes, explicit indexing, compound arithmetic, and pointer-wrapper spellings retained that result. Visible recursive override resolvers changed the initial comparison but also removed reloads or introduced additional resolution steps, so those larger bodies were rejected. Every trial source and its unedited probe output is retained under build/0015a600-finish/; measurements.jsonl records the hypotheses and results, and build/verdict.txt records progress.

The decisive hypothesis was that the two object-template expressions originally passed through `Thing::getTemplate`, rather than through a manually expanded chain walk. The already-matched definition in `game/GameEngine/Source/Common/Thing/Thing_isKindOf.cpp` supplies the actual helper, including its separate early null return. This is different from merely wrapping the bank's combined condition in a pointer-returning helper. The hypothesis would have been refuted if the real helper retained the comparison and register mirror, or if its independent retail extent failed verification.

Using that helper through canonical Thing and Object declarations reduces the caller to five differences, all in the reversed zero stores. Reversing the chained initialization then makes the body exact. Separate initialized declarations also independently produce the exact body in this context. The earlier initialization trials are therefore insufficient evidence of a compiler limitation: their manual template expressions had a different allocation result. Raw measurements are build/0015a600-finish/01-bank-raw.probe.log, build/0015a600-finish/90-proven-thing-getter.probe.log, build/0015a600-finish/100-reverse-chain.probe.log, and build/0015a600-finish/103-native-counter-definitions.probe.log.
