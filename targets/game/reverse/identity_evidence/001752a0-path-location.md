## Boundary and caller evidence

The decoded body returns at offsets +0x3C, +0x293, +0x369 and +0x3AE. Every conditional branch stays inside the extent, there is no outgoing tail jump, and INT3 padding begins at +0x3AF. The complete decoded callers are AIAttackMeleeEngageState::computePath at RVA 0x00177A90 (952 bytes) and the anonymous body at RVA 0x00182F70 (1107 bytes). Their calls at +0x309 and +0x2E4 reach ILT 0x0001E0D3, which jumps to this body. They push victim, source and destination in that order, discard twelve argument bytes after the call and test AL. The target reads those three pointer slots, has no incoming receiver or hidden result pointer, and returns with plain RET. This proves the three-argument cdecl shape and a one-byte predicate result.

The full instruction listings and checked inventories are retained in build/1752a0-finish/retail-decodes.log, checked-target.log, checked-caller1.log and checked-caller2.log. A different thunk destination, an escaping branch, an additional return path or a caller with a different argument setup would refute this boundary or ABI account.
