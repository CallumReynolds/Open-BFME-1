## The successful hypothesis

The typed EDX drawable adapter forces its vtable load before argument materialization. A native second drawable query restores the complete FX argument schedule, but by itself changes the following pathfinder receiver load from EAX to ECX. That longer global-load encoding shifts later instructions and makes its raw difference count worse. Supplying the in-class pathfinder accessor restores the receiver expression structure and removes that drift. The accessor follows the GeneralsMD AI::pathfinder() body; the actual retail instructions independently establish the singleton's member offset +0x0C.

This hypothesis would have been refuted if the FX push schedule or pathfinder global-load register still differed after combining the two native operations. build/weapon001e9380/028-native-second-speed-temporary.log shows the intermediate regression; build/weapon001e9380/033-native-pathfinder-accessor.log shows the improvement.
