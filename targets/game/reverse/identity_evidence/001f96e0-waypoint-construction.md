## Retry hypothesis and experiments

The reproduced bank emits 979 bytes with 150 differences outside object relocation fields and two relocation sites that do not align with retail instructions. The first byte difference is +0x50, the displacement operand of the instruction at +0x4D. The full comparison locates the rotated module, coordinate-copy, destination-slot and vector locals, the early +0x1F8 flag transfer, the last-element expression at +0x2D2, the link-loop limit at +0x322 and the final array-cleanup register. Raw evidence is build/001f96e0-finish/baseline.probe.log and listing.cod. The hypothesis is that evaluation order and the lifetime of reloaded node pointers, rather than a forced register assignment, account for this coupled residue. A preserved difference after the corresponding source changes would refute each experiment.

Empty coordinate construction, default construction followed by assignment, fieldwise coordinate initialization, enclosing lexical scopes, a scoped native vector-base name, and visibility of the complete existing Waypoint constructor did not improve the bank. Their exact trial sources and outputs are indexed by build/001f96e0-finish/experiments.jsonl. The generated store and EH choices were inspected against the complete historical verdicts and were not rerun as another unchanged search.

Evaluating `path[path.size() - 1]` before initializing `count` reduces the difference count to 72. Explicitly retaining the first node pointer across its byte store and then separately reloading the next-node pointer reduces that trial to 44 and restores retail's stack slots. Expressing the exclusion-record update and subsequent Boolean update through inline members reduces the evaluation-order trial to 28. Combining the three changes reproduces all 979 bytes outside relocation operands. Raw measurements are indexed-last-before-count.probe.log, count-loop-next-pointer.probe.log, native-mask-setters.probe.log and combined-setters-pointers.probe.log in the same build directory.
