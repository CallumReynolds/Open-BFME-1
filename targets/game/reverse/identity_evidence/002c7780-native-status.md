## Hypothesis and measured result

The saved reconstruction reproduces its earlier measurement: 565 emitted bytes, 193 masked differences and one surplus byte. Retail holds the secondary receiver in EBP and the module-data pointer, subsequently reused for position, in EDI. The bank reverses those registers. Its EBP-based position load at retail offset +0x15D needs an extra displacement byte, shifting the remaining instructions and ten relocation operands. The earlier label of register allocation described the emitted symptom rather than its source cause.

The successful hypothesis was that the status test also needs the native BitFlags accessor layer. The bank already used STLport for the model-condition test but manually read the status word. The independently decoded status mutator at 0x001C7370 reads and writes all three words at Object offsets +0x90, +0x94 and +0x98; the complete single-bit helper at 0x000D3EB0 constructs the same three-word mask and forwards it. The source uses `BitFlags<86>::test(3)` over that canonical backing storage. An unchanged receiver allocation, changed bit-test width or incorrect accessed word would refute this hypothesis. Instead this single source operation restores the retail register allocation and removes every byte difference. No dynamic STL container, key or payload is reconstructed here.

Raw measurements are in build/wander-002c7780-rerun/baseline-raw.log, flags-native-status.log and final-source-probe.log. Every trial source and its unedited probe output are retained in that directory. build/verdict.txt records the experiment hypotheses and measured results. The runner and audit scripts there reproduce the probes and assertions.

Rejected alternatives include retained versus reloaded owner pointers, an explicit position-pointer lifetime, delayed module-data lifetime, query argument temporaries, native Thing-to-Object getter layering, signed versus unsigned versus enum condition indices, byte versus bool fields, native aggregate operations with pointer/reference/value boundaries, split guards, and split secondary-base views. Authentic visible movement and filter-link helpers did not alter the caller. The generated copy-family search reported no applicable source transformation. Earlier recorded EH, constructor-visibility and local-order experiments were not used as a claim of exhaustion. Removing the owner reload for vision changes the initial register allocation but removes an operation retail performs, so that candidate is rejected.
