## Boundary, arguments and string representation

The complete target occupies `[0x0035A060, 0x0035A1BE)`. It has one common bare `ret` at target offset `+0x15D`. All conditional branches stay inside that extent. The backward branch at `+0x126` reaches `+0x45`, where the suffix is restored before the next formatting iteration. There is no external tail jump. Raw evidence is `build/0035a060-finish/decoded-initial.log` and `build/0035a060-finish/checked-target.log`.

The complete caller at `0x0035DD40` pushes the address of its local string, loads the owner from `[esi+0x0C]` into ECX and calls the target at caller offset `+0x3E`. It removes four stack bytes after the call. The target reads that stack argument at entry offset `+0x0E`, saves ECX in EBX and ends with a bare return. This establishes the observed private convention: ECX carries the owner and one caller-cleaned stack word carries the string pointer. It does not establish an original owner class or method name. The source keeps `rvaUniqueName0035a060` and the bank's existing address-derived layout names. The caller's complete decode and inventory are in `decoded-initial.log` and `checked-caller.log` in the same raw-evidence directory.

Its complete decode shows a stack-passed destination for the returned object, its copy construction, EAX returning that destination and `ret 4`. The target's formatting constructor at `0x00888BC0` scans single bytes, and its inlined comparison zero-extends a 16-bit length at buffer offset `+4` and reads bytes at buffer offset `+8`. The pre-run bank's cast to `UnicodeString` therefore does not establish a wide-string operation. Replacing it with `name->set(candidate)` preserves the emitted body. | Retail region | Explained difference |

| `+0x2E` and `+0xA1` | Forward displacements change because the compiled tail is shorter. |
| `+0xA7` through `+0xBC` | Retail loads the record index into ECX, computes the byte offset in EBP and spills the scalar index at `[esp+0x1C]`. The bank loads the index into EAX and caches the complete record pointer in that stack slot. |
| `+0xBF` through `+0x105` | Retail retains the candidate length in EDX as well as `[esp+0x14]`, and the record's own length in EAX. The bank uses different registers and reloads the candidate length. These live ranges follow from retaining a record pointer rather than a record offset. |
| `+0x106` through `+0x12B` | Retail merges the intrinsic comparison result into EAX, then tests the scalar index, reloads the array from `[ebx+0x0C]` and reads the released byte through the retained offset. The bank branches directly on comparison results and reads the released byte from its cached record pointer. |
| `+0x12C` through `+0x15D` | The assignment receiver and argument-address loads have the opposite order, and the shorter comparison and reload sequence shifts the destructor and epilogue. The final five retail offsets have no emitted counterpart. |

## Rejected experiments

The two pre-run immutable archived reconstructions were inspected. Their reconstruction bodies are identical to the pre-run bank after normalizing the old `Code/` include path; their score labels do not describe distinct compiler shapes. The raw source comparison is `history.log`.

The retry hypothesis was that helper visibility or the native parent/table expression could retain the retail reload while preserving the ECX-owner convention. It is refuted for the tested forms: complete visible predicate and owner-member lookup forms reload through the owner but pass the owner on the stack; table-only forms retain ECX but cache the record pointer. Moving the rebase ahead of the initial guard changes allocation and spill placement without matching. The complete visible lower-bound helper independently reproduces all 208 helper bytes and leaves the target unchanged.
