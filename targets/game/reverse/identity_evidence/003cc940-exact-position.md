Its first wrong byte is +0x5C, and it needs an extra `fxch st(1)` at candidate +0x14D. The complete earlier records describe isolated precision, first-assignment and declaration swaps, rather than establishing that every combination of those changes was exhausted.

It emits exactly 437 bytes with six differences, beginning at +0x183. It reproduces every target x87 instruction, operand and branch. This refutes the earlier diagnosis that the X residue was compiler-internal or needed assembly.
