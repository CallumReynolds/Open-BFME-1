## Identity and declarations

EA's aligned name record for this address is `Pathfinder::NewMap`. The ILT oracle confirms `?NewMap@Pathfinder@@QAEXXZ` and contradicts the bank's lowercase `?newMap@Pathfinder@@QAEXXZ`. The receiver fields are the BFME pathfinder layout: map storage at +0x0C and +0x10, integer extent at +0x14 through +0x20, sixteen 0x44-byte layers beginning at +0x85C, and zone manager at +0xC9C. The Zero Hour `Pathfinder::newMap` donor has the same extent, allocation, layer and object-pass structure; BFME replaces its wall-layer block with waypoint association. These independent observations support the EA spelling. A decoded caller using another receiver, or a different ILT destination for this address, would refute the identity.

The source includes the canonical `Lib/Coord3D.h`. Its local Pathfinder and PathfindLayer declarations follow decoded BFME offsets, which differ from the Zero Hour header. The existing pathfinding shim also documents these offsets but does not declare NewMap, allocation members, the zone manager, or the constructor and destructor needed by this target. In particular, name_oracle reports the old m_blockOfMapCells witness at +0x08, whereas retail stores the allocated cell pointer at +0x0C and writes the ready byte at +0x08. The local view follows the decoded stores. No shared header changes are required. Existing descriptive bank names are retained apart from the oracle-confirmed EA capitalization of NewMap.

Raw outputs: build/newmap-003d80f0/02-visible-world-probe.log and build/newmap-003d80f0/03-visible-layer-probe.log.
